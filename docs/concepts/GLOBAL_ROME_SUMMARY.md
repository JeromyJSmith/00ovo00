## The App: How It Works

The core mechanic is beautifully simple:

1. **Each person gets a virtual knob** on their phone (200–800 Hz range)
2. **Turning the knob broadcasts their frequency** via Supabase Realtime to all connected users
3. **Each participant appears as a spinning triangle** — spin direction based on frequency (< 430 Hz = counter-clockwise, > 430 Hz = clockwise)
4. **When 80%+ of participants align within 5 Hz** → harmonic resonance is achieved
5. **At resonance (730 Hz lock)**: all triangles flip direction simultaneously, a harmonic tone plays across every device, Rome's photo appears with the word "NOW"
6. **Each triangle is minted as an NFT** (ERC-1155 on Polygon) — a permanent blockchain record of the moment

For the memorial: 47 people in the Rose Room, phones connected, triangles projected on the wall. The room collectively *tunes* to resonance. Rome's theory, proven live.
