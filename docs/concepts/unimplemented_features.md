# Unimplemented Features — Global Ohm Ecosystem

## Ceremony & Experience

### Ceremony Phases
A structured orchestration of the collective experience:
1. **Gathering** — users explore their individual frequencies
2. **Convergence** — clusters form as users gravitate toward each other
3. **The Flip / Collective Breath** — all triangles synchronize, trigger unified "NOW" sweep
4. **Dispersal & Memory** — triangles fade, leave a permanent star point

### Sacred Geometry Formations
When the swarm achieves 80% resonance lock at 730 Hz, individual spinning triangles self-organize into massive, perfect geometric patterns: **Flower of Life** or **Metatron's Cube**.

### Solo Meditation Mode
Individual practice: user's solitary triangle floats in a void. As they tune to 730 Hz, the app plays a random teaching from Rome.

## Social Features

### Lock-On Mode
Tap another person's triangle to "couple" frequencies. Draws a visual **beam of light** connecting the two users.

### Conflict Resolution Mode
Two users in an argument open the app holding opposite frequencies (e.g., 220 Hz vs. 730 Hz). The system guides them to meet at the **500 Hz neutral state**, where they must write out their shared truth.

### The Great Game Multiplayer Mode
Up to **100 users** attempt to reach the 80% collective alignment threshold as quickly as possible, communicating **only through frequency control** (no voice chat).

### Autopilot Mode
Automatically and smoothly turns a user's virtual knob to gravitate their triangle toward the dominant frequency cluster.

## Interactive & Chaos

### Chaos Mode (Easter Egg)
Violently shaking the phone makes the user's triangle spin wildly, change frequencies randomly, and emit sparks. Visually represents bad-faith actors before the algorithm naturally decays their influence.

## Biometric Integration

### Breath Detection
Uses the phone's microphone or capacitive touch sensor to detect the user's physical exhale. The breath duration controls the length of the **"O" (sustain) phase** during the collective N-O-W sweep.
